Where each icon here comes from.

These are Elden Ring's own icons, art copyright FromSoftware, Inc. and
Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc., copied from the Elden Ring wiki at
https://eldenring.wiki.gg. The wiki tags most of the files as copyrighted
game content used under fair use, and leaves a few with no tag; none is
licensed for reuse. They are shown here as fan sites and tools show them, so
players recognise them at a glance.
